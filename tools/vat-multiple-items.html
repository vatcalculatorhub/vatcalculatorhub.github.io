VAT on Multiple Items Calculator

Add up the VAT on three items at once, using one rate for all of them.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

If your items have different rates, calculate each one separately.

How this calculator works

Each item's VAT is its net price multiplied by the rate. The totals are the sums of the net prices, the VAT amounts and the gross prices.

Worked example

Items 50, 80 and 120 at an example rate of 20%: total net 250, total VAT 50, total gross 300.
