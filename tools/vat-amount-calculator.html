VAT Amount Calculator

Work out the VAT amount on a net price at the rate you enter.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Enter the rate that applies in your country and to your item.

How this calculator works

The VAT amount is the net price multiplied by the VAT rate as a decimal.

Worked example

Net 250 at an example rate of 20%: VAT is 50 and the gross price is 300.
