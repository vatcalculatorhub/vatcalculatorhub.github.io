Frequently asked questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by the VAT rate and add the result to the net price. The calculator does this for you.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the gross price by one plus the VAT rate. For an example rate of 20%, divide by 1.20.

What VAT rate should I use?

Use the rate that applies in your country and to the item you are pricing. Rates vary by country and by item, so check your own source.

Is VAT the same as sales tax?

They are similar in that both are taxes on purchases, but VAT is collected at each stage of production and sale, while sales tax is usually charged only at the final sale.

Do all items have VAT?

No. Many countries zero-rate or exempt some items, such as certain foods or medicines. The treatment varies by country.

Can a business reclaim VAT?

A registered business can usually reclaim the VAT it pays on purchases, and it passes on the VAT it charges. The exact rules vary by country.

What is the difference between net and gross?

Net is the price before VAT, gross is the price after VAT is added.

How do I calculate VAT on several items?

Add the net prices together and apply the rate once, if all items share the same rate. If the rates differ, calculate each item separately.
