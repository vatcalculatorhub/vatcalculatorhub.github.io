About

VAT Calculator is a free, practical calculator site. It was built to answer one question clearly: what does this cost, how long will it take, or how much do I need? Every tool on the site works entirely in your browser, using the numbers you enter. Nothing you type is sent to a server, stored, or shared.

The guides on this site explain the calculation behind each tool in plain words, with worked examples using real numbers. Where a guide mentions a rate or a price, it is labelled as an example, because rates and prices vary by country, provider and time. Always use the rate or price from your own bill, contract or local provider for a decision that matters.

This site is maintained as a helpful reference. It does not provide financial, tax, legal or professional advice. For a decision with significant money or legal consequences, check with a qualified professional in your country.
